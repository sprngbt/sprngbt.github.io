Installation steps

  1. Plug your Springbot into a USB-C port.
  2. Hit one of the install buttons below and select the correct serial port.
  3. If connection is troublesome, put the Springbot into download mode — on the back of the Springbot:
    • Press and keep holding ▶ down.
    • Press ↻ button.
    • Release ▶ button.
    • Back LED ☀ will continuously illuminate.
    • When firmware installed, reset the Springbot by pressing ↻ button.
